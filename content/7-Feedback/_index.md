In this section, I would like to share some personal feelings, perspectives, and feedback after participating in the First Cloud Journey program at Amazon Web Services Vietnam Company Limited. As a Cloud Infrastructure Engineer in the project **Smart Healthcare Appointment System**, I had unique experiences and valuable lessons while building an online medical appointment booking system.

### General Evaluation

#### 1. Learning and Working Environment

The program environment is friendly, open, and promotes a strong self-learning spirit. The roadmap is divided into distinct stages, helping me track progress more easily and identify focus areas at each phase. At the same time, the technical requirements of a healthcare-oriented project demanded a higher level of precision regarding system security, network design, and service availability.

Through labs, workshops, and practical activities, I had the opportunity to get acquainted with many groups of AWS services. Directly operating in a cloud environment helped me realize that designing a secure network architecture is not only a technical concern but also a core requirement when handling sensitive patient-related data.

I believe the program could become even stronger by organizing more specialized workshops for specific domains such as FinTech, HealthTech, or e-commerce so participants can gain deeper practical perspectives from different real-world industry use cases.

#### 2. Mentor and Admin Support

My mentor accompanied and strongly supported me in troubleshooting complex network configurations, executing labs, and deploying the **Smart Healthcare Appointment System** project. The team admin also maintained clear communication, shared the necessary information on time, and helped me manage my schedule more effectively so I could complete assigned tasks on track.

What I appreciated most was that support did not stop at simply giving the final answer. Instead, I was often guided step by step, encouraged to investigate the problem on my own, and then discuss possible solutions. This made the learning process much deeper and helped me build more confidence when facing technical issues.

#### 3. Suitability with Professional Background

The internship aligned very well with my development direction in cloud infrastructure. Fundamental knowledge of computer networks, databases, and security that I learned at university could be directly applied when building the medical appointment management system.

Designing the infrastructure required careful thinking about network partitioning through VPC and subnet design, along with controlling access through Security Groups, IAM, and storage policies. Because of that, I could clearly see the connection between academic theory and real operational requirements in a production-oriented environment.

#### 4. Learning and Skill Development Opportunities

The program created comprehensive development opportunities not only in technical knowledge but also in technical documentation, requirement analysis, and the ability to translate abstract ideas into practical infrastructure architecture.

I proactively combined official AWS documentation, community-shared resources, and AI-assisted research tools to optimize the learning process and solve challenges that appeared during system setup. This way of learning helped me become more independent and more efficient when approaching new services or unfamiliar concepts.

#### 5. Culture and Teamwork

The program created a connected and supportive community environment. The enthusiastic support from other members when I encountered issues related to storage, security, or service configuration made me appreciate the importance of teamwork in technology projects.

Even though each person worked on different tasks, there was still a strong sense of collaboration through knowledge sharing, problem-solving discussions, and mutual encouragement. This helped me understand that technical growth is much faster when it is supported by a healthy team culture.

#### 6. Support Policies and Learning Resources

The program materials and lab system provided a strong starting point for self-study and practical implementation. They helped me move from theory to hands-on work in a structured way.

However, I think the learning resources could be even more useful if they included more troubleshooting scenarios, incident simulation exercises, or short post-mortem style discussions. These additions would help trainees sharpen their reflexes when facing real system failures instead of only following ideal paths.

### Most Satisfying Aspect

What satisfied me most was the ability to connect many individual AWS services from separate labs into a more complete system for the **Smart Healthcare Appointment System** project. This gave me a much more realistic understanding of how cloud components interact, support one another, and contribute to solving actual business problems.

In addition, community events with open and practical sharing from energetic speakers inspired me to continue pursuing a deeper technical path in cloud infrastructure and DevOps.

### Suggested Improvements

Based on my practical experience, I think the program could be further improved through the following points:

- Add more specialized guidelines on data security standards for domain-specific systems such as healthcare and finance.
- Increase demo sessions or troubleshooting and post-mortem sharing from mentors.
- Provide standardized templates for architecture diagrams and technical reporting earlier in the program.
- Include more real-world scenarios involving system scaling, cost control, and service failure handling.

### Program Introduction

I am fully ready to recommend this program to anyone who wants to enter the fields of cloud computing and AWS. The program has high practical value, requires serious self-learning, and creates a strong foundation for both technical growth and professional orientation.

Although the journey can be challenging, especially for learners who are new to cloud environments, the results are worthwhile. The program not only helps participants understand AWS services more clearly, but also teaches them how to think in terms of architecture, security, scalability, and long-term operational efficiency.
