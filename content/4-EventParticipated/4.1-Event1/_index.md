### What Was Learned

#### Design Thinking

- **Business-first approach**: Always start from the business domain, not technology.
- **Ubiquitous language**: Importance of a common vocabulary between business and tech teams.
- **Bounded contexts**: How to identify and manage complexity in large systems.

#### Technical Architecture

- **Event storming technique**: A practical method to model business processes.
- Use **event-driven communication** instead of only synchronous calls.
- **Integration patterns**: Understand when to use sync, async, pub/sub, and streaming.
- **Compute spectrum**: Selection criteria from VM to containers to serverless.

#### Modernization Strategy

- **Phased approach**: Do not rush; there must be a clear roadmap.
- **7Rs framework**: Different pathways depending on each application's characteristics.
- **ROI measurement**: Cost reduction combined with business agility.

### Work Application

- Apply DDD thinking to the current project through event storming sessions.
- Refactor service boundaries using bounded contexts.
- Replace selected synchronous calls with asynchronous messaging patterns.
- Pilot serverless approaches such as AWS Lambda where suitable.
- Explore integrating Amazon Q Developer into the daily development workflow.
